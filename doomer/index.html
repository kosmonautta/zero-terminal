KIND-II OPERATORDOOMER

ULTRABLACK OPERATOR

Kindling is not phase reversal. A Flare is not a reversed Yoke — it is what a Yoke leaves behind when it's driven past its own threshold: trace ESP, residual and spent. KIND-II ESP has never touched Focal Phase and never will. The Doomer is Liminal, permanently. What the Doomer lacks is not power, but the door an ESPer has — the ability to phase-reverse back toward lighter, Focal expression. That door does not exist here, and there is no idle setting on what remains: Doomer output runs redlined by default, every reading pinned to the edge of what the body can discharge before something in the Operant's ledger has to give.

§1.2 The Doomer channels and discharges ESP without possessing the refinement Kind-I Operators exhibit. The Doomer does not build. The Doomer takes.

§1.3 Absence of refinement is not absence of yield. Doomer output trades frequency for magnitude. Foundational gaps recorded under [REDACTED].

§1.4 Liminal Phase is the only phase a KIND-II Operator's ESP occupies — origin and terminus both. Focal Phase, and the return path to it, belongs to Kind-I Operators alone.

ARTICLE III — THE LIMINAL BINARY

§3.1 KIND-II Operators have no access to Focal Phase. The stabilizing apparatus that permits Hex Grave — and the return path to Focal Phase an ESPer relies on — is structurally absent in Doomer physiology. The Doomer is Liminal, permanently.

§3.2 KIND-II ESP exists only within the Liminal binary: Yoke, the base liminal form drawn directly from static, and Flare, the trace residue thrown off when that same Yoke is Kindled past its threshold. Neither end of this binary touches Focal Phase.

§3.3 The binary trades predictability for magnitude. Doomer output is fewer in number and greater in consequence than Kind-I output.

§3.4 Kindling does not reverse phase. There is none left to reverse into, and KIND-II ESP was never Focal to begin with. Kindling escalates: a Fixed Yoke driven past its own threshold, burning off as a Flare — trace residual ESP, not a mirrored form of the Yoke it came from.

DOOMER FIELD PROCEDURE

On Nicking, Kindling, and the cost of a Flare Fixed

YOKE / FLARE PROCEDURE DIRECTIVES

Nicking

A Yoke is Nicked during Finetuning — mid-mission, not before it. There is no briefing stage for a Doomer, no study, no preparation ahead of the theft. The operator forces one Signal and one Noise into agreement in that same motion and the static yields, or it does not. A Nicked Yoke occupies one inventory slot. Failed Nicking attempts cost the action and nothing else — the static simply refuses to close.


Fixing (the Yoke)

At any point, during the operator's turn, they can Fix a Yoke with a Half-Action. This is its use. Fixing a Yoke does not remove it from inventory, but it cannot be Fixed again until it has been Kindled.


Kindling

After a Yoke has been Fixed, the operator may choose to Kindle it. This is not phase reversal — the Yoke was never Focal, and what spills out of it isn't either. Kindling drives an already-liminal Signal and Noise past their own threshold, burning the excess off as a Flare: trace residual ESP thrown clear of the spent Yoke, not a mirror of it. Kindling is a Full Action. There is no Rite Action and no Observation Action available to a Doomer; Kindling either completes on this turn or the Yoke is spent for nothing.


Fixing (the Flare)

A Kindled Flare must be Fixed to resolve. Fixing a Flare always pays its Cost in full, immediately, regardless of outcome. There is no partial payment and no refusal once Kindling has completed. A Flare Fixed does not settle back toward the Yoke's base state — there is no lighter state left to settle toward. The Yoke beneath it is spent; what's left is residue only.

YOKE FINETUNING

A Yoke is not rolled blind. Nicking happens here, in Finetuning — one Signal forced against one Noise, chosen by the Doomer in the same breath the theft closes; the static offers no other axes. Signal is the shape of the force itself: a SPIKE is a singular point of penetration or projection, a LOOP is recurrence and sustained continuation, a SEAM is junction and transition between states, a LENS is direction, concentration, and convergence. Noise is what the static does with it once forced: ECHO reflects and propagates it, DRIFT displaces and destabilizes it, SCAR leaves it as persistent, permanent imprint, NULL suppresses and erases it outright.

A Yoke is named by its pair: Signal over Noise. SPIKE / ECHO. LENS / NULL. Sixteen possible pairs. Each is a debt not yet called.
